How KritZaar Games Works

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Two kinds of games, kept apart

KritZaar Originals are made by KritZaar. We own them, host them, and they live in their own folder of the site. Web games from Playgama are embedded from Playgama's own servers and are not stored by KritZaar. Everything else is third-party: KritZaar does not store, host, or distribute those game files. We provide a player and a catalog of guides that point to each game's official source.

You bring your own legal copy

  1. Pick a game in the catalog and read its page.
  2. Follow the link to the official source (the publisher, developer, or an official store) and download the file there.
  3. Come back to KritZaar and drop the file into the box. KritZaar looks at the file and works out the type (DOS, NES, Game Boy, Flash) by itself.
  4. The game is saved in your browser and plays on your device, even offline.

Only use files you have the right to use, such as freeware, shareware, open-source, homebrew, or games you own.

Your files stay on your device

Game files and their file names never leave your device. They are not uploaded and not logged. They live in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) and only you can open them.

Keeping your saves safe

Browsers can clear website storage, especially on iPhone and iPad if you do not visit for about a week. Add KritZaar to your home screen and use the backup option in My Games to export your games and saves.

When a game does not work

If a game cannot start, the player says why in plain words. You can press "Report this problem" to get a short report (game, error, browser details; never the game file or your saves). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. Each catalog entry also shows whether we have tested it.
